Hi, Grace here 👋🏼

I’m a combined master’s/Ph.D. student in Interaction Science at Sungkyunkwan University, specializing in human–AI interaction. My advisor is Prof. Changjun Lee, and I’m part of AXIS Lab, affectionately known as “✨ Prof. Lee’s Jewel Box✨”

After earning a B.F.A. in Photography and Photographic Images, I worked with public-sector organizations to develop and evaluate policies for culture and the arts.

I bring this interdisciplinary and applied perspective to understanding the mechanisms behind users’ behavior and decisions in human–AI interaction. I’m particularly interested in how people form expectations about AI, interpret its responses, and decide when to rely on it. I also examine how the context of an interaction shapes their trust, confidence, and choices.

Research interests

  • Human-AI Interaction
  • AI-mediated Decision-Making
  • Societal Impacts of AI
  • Responsible AI
  • AI Policy & Governance
