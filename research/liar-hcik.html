Liar Game in an AI Crowd

Social support and heuristic cues in a group of AI agents.

Status
Published · Conference Paper
Authorship
Co-author

Overview

This study explores persuasion and psychological responses in a discussion between a human participant and multiple AI agents.

Approach

A text-based Liar Game paired one human with five LLM agents. A factorial experiment varied social support and message strength.

Findings

Social support increased perceived persuasiveness. The abstract reports no main effect of message strength on persuasion, while weaker messages increased perceived social pressure. The study discusses how users’ expectations shape experiences of AI groups.
