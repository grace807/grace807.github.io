Who’s the Liar?

Convergent AI opinions, message strength, and judgment under ambiguity.

Status
Published · Conference Paper
Authorship
Co-Author

Overview

This pilot study examines how people respond when several AI agents converge on an opinion in an ambiguous discussion.

Approach

A Liar Game task compared confident, explicitly reasoned AI messages with hedged, hesitant messages.

Findings

Directional patterns suggested that stronger messages increased persuasion, social pressure, switching toward the AI-designated target, and confidence. Responses varied with attitude clarity. These are preliminary findings, rather than definitive evidence of a general effect.
