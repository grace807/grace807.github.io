Social media as a journalistic environment

Platform attributes, perceived journalistic roles, and selective reinforcement.

Status
Published · Journal Article
Authorship
Co-author

Overview

This study examines how journalistic attributes of social media relate to users’ responses to information that aligns or conflicts with their beliefs.

Approach

Platform assessments were combined with survey data from 993 users. Mediation models examined immediacy, public sphere, agenda setting, and transparency.

Findings

All four attributes showed negative indirect associations with selective reinforcement through perceived journalistic role. The findings highlight the relevance of users’ interpretations of platforms, alongside platform features themselves.
